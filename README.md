# Public Servant Information in Bangladesh

## Public Nature of Information

In Bangladesh, information regarding public servants is generally considered public information under the **Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2009**.

### Key Points:
1.  **Right to Information Act (RTI) 2009**: This act empowers citizens to request information from public authorities. It covers government organizations and their activities.
2.  **Public Directories**: The Government of Bangladesh maintains a National Portal (bangladesh.gov.bd) where contact details (Name, Designation, Phone, Email) of public servants across various ministries and departments are often publicly listed.
3.  **Asset Declarations**: While public servants are required to declare assets to the government, the public availability of these specific records has been a subject of debate and varies by specific context and legal interpretation.
4.  **Official Acts**: Decisions and official acts performed in the capacity of a public servant are public records.

However, private personal information (like personal family details, private biometric data) that is not related to public duties is generally protected.
